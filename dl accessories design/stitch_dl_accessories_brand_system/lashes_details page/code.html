Lash Care Guide expand_more

1. Gently remove from the tray starting from the outer corner.

2. Measure against your eye and trim the band if necessary.

3. Apply a thin layer of adhesive and wait 30 seconds to become tacky.

4. Place as close to your natural lash line as possible.
